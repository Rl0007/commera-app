Motion samples: icons and ERPNext sync

Two ideas, a few variants each. Scroll slowly; every sample starts when it enters the screen. With reduced motion on, each one shows its final frame.

Idea 1 · Self-drawing icons

Variant 1a

Feature row, staggered draw

Four outline icons draw stroke by stroke, left to right, as the row scrolls in.

Storefront

Cart and checkout, ready to sell.

ERPNext books

Sales Orders and invoices, made for you.

Payments

Razorpay, Stripe, Telr, Tabby or cash on delivery.

Shipping

Labels and tracking with Shiprocket or AfterShip.

Variant 1b

Draw, then a colour pop

Each icon draws in ink, then a soft disc pops behind it and the stroke warms to its accent with a small bounce.

Payments

Variant 1c

Icon draws while its label types in

The heading types letter by letter beside the drawing icon, then the description rises line by line.

Sell online

A storefront and checkout that write straight into ERPNext.

Books that balance

Prepaid orders get a Sales Invoice and a Payment Entry.

Ship the same day

Book couriers and track parcels through Shiprocket or AfterShip.

Idea 2 · Order flows into ERPNext

Variant 2a

Scrubbed with scroll

Scroll drives it: the order is placed, the connector draws, the chip travels and the ledger rows tick in. Scroll back to rewind.

Variant 2b

Plays on enter, then loops gently

Runs once when it scrolls in, then repeats every few seconds with the next prepaid order.

Variant 2c

Orders streaming in

A steady stream of chips; every arrival counts a Sales Order, and prepaid ones also count an invoice and a payment.
