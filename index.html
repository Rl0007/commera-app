Questions

Do I need ERPNext?+

Yes. Commera runs on top of ERPNext, which keeps your stock, prices, tax and accounts.

Is it free?+

The code is open and free to download. You host it on your own server or on Frappe Cloud. Payment and shipping providers charge their own fees.

Can I use my own design?+

Yes. Pick a theme, or have a designer restyle any page, checkout included.

Which countries does it support?+

Payments cover India (Razorpay), the GCC and MENA (Telr, Tabby) and worldwide cards (Stripe). Shipping covers India (Shiprocket) and global carriers (AfterShip).
