Storefront

Fast pages your shoppers can find.

Server-rendered with Jinja, Tailwind and Alpine. There's no app bundle between a shopper and a product page.

  • Themes without forking. A theme can restyle any page, checkout included.
  • Bilingual and RTL-ready. English and Arabic from the URL, with right-to-left layout throughout.
  • Built for search. Sitemap, per-page metadata, llms.txt and generated Open Graph images.

Merchant dashboard

Run the shop without opening Desk.

A Vue 3 and Frappe UI app at /commera. It speaks products, collections and orders, not warehouses and naming series.

  • Orders. Unfulfilled, unpaid, open and closed, with payment and fulfilment state on each.
  • Products. Variants, pricing, stock, bulk publishing and bulk image upload.
  • Customers and reviews. Lifetime spend per customer, and reviews you publish yourself.
  • Analytics. Revenue, inventory and a first-party storefront funnel.

Questions

Do I need ERPNext?+

Yes. Commera is a Frappe app that runs on top of ERPNext, which stays the system of record for stock, pricing, tax and accounts.

Is it free?+

The code is open on GitHub and you host it yourself, like any other Frappe app. Payment and shipping providers charge their own fees.

Can I use my own design?+

Yes. Themes are data plus a template folder, and a theme can override any page the app ships, so you restyle without forking.

Which countries does it support?+

Payments cover India (Razorpay), the GCC and MENA (Telr, Tabby) and worldwide cards (Stripe). Shipping covers India (Shiprocket) and global carriers (AfterShip).
